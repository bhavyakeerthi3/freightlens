How the analysis works

Clear arithmetic, explicit decisions and reproducible evidence.

01

Measure fairly

Weekly rate = total freight cost ÷ total tonne-kilometres. Group by direction, supplied route type and Monday week date.

Σ freight / Σ (tonnes × km)
02

Compare precisely

History is the mean of observed weekly rates in the preceding eight calendar weeks. Peers are other routes of the same type in the same week.

No current-week history. No self in peer averages. Missing baselines stay unavailable.

03

Flag with a clear rule

The rate must rise from the prior observed week and exceed history or peers by more than 20%. This policy can miss gradual rises and expensive plateaus.

04

Explain with evidence

Reviewed note text, route, overlapping dates and cost impact gate local TF-IDF retrieval. Unsupported changes remain unexplained.

Evidence supports an impact; it does not prove the full increase is fair.
